Tattoo stencils, explained

A tattoo stencil maker that keeps your line.

What is a tattoo stencil?

A tattoo stencil, also called a tattoo transfer or tattoo template, is the outline of a design printed so the artist can place it on skin before the first needle touches it. A good stencil keeps the line weight of the original, drops the paper texture and shadows, and is exactly the size the tattoo will be.

How to turn a drawing or photo into a tattoo stencil

Load a scan, a flash sheet or a phone photo of your design. Skinstencil traces the lines in your browser, and you adjust sensitivity, smoothing and line weight until the preview matches your drawing. Switch between drawing mode for ink on paper and photo mode for pictures with tone. The result is a clean black line on white, ready to export.

Print at true size, on thermal or regular paper

Set the width in millimetres and the print page opens the stencil at exactly that size. Unlocked downloads are up to 4000 px and carry a 300 dpi size inside the PNG, so the right size comes through in your print software. Use it with a thermal stencil printer or print it and trace it onto transfer paper.

Made for tattoo artists, apprentices and clients with their own design

Use it for flash, lettering, linework, mandalas and custom pieces. Stencils are made on your device and your images are not uploaded. The free tool carries a watermark and downloads up to 800 px; a day pass or Pro removes the watermark and lifts the size limit.

Questions

Before you try it.

Is it really free?

Yes. The tool in the studio is free and has no sign-up. Downloads from the free tool carry a small watermark and are limited to 800 pixels. A day pass or Pro removes the watermark and unlocks the full size.

How do I remove the watermark?

Pick a day pass or Pro and pay at the secure checkout. You get a license key by email. Paste it into the studio and the watermark is gone. The key is checked with our payment provider and kept only in your browser.

Is this AI?

The stencil filter is classic image processing: it finds lines by edges and brightness, removes specks and sets the line weight. That keeps it fast, private and predictable on clean drawings.

Do you keep my designs?

No. The free tool runs in your browser and does not upload your images. Closing the page discards them.

Can I upload any tattoo?

Only designs you made or have the right to use. We remove material when a rights holder asks.

Which files work?

JPG, PNG and WebP. Large images are scaled down to keep the tool fast. A flat, well-lit photo of a drawing works well.

How do I print a stencil?

Download the PNG and print it at the size you set. Paid files carry the 300 dpi size, so print software uses true size. Thermal stencil printers print onto thermal transfer paper. Inkjet printers need stencil ink and inkjet transfer paper.

What about a full sleeve or a big piece?

Work motif by motif. Smaller parts keep their detail much better than one huge image.

Does it work on a tablet or phone?

Yes, in the browser. A phone app may follow.

Why does it drop my fine hatching?

Lower sensitivity and higher speck removal drop fine lines on purpose. Raise sensitivity, lower speck removal, and check the drift overlay to see what was lost.

Can I get a refund?

The day pass is not refundable, because the license key works as soon as you receive it. Pro can be refunded in full within 14 days of your first payment: write to hello@skinstencil.com. Renewals are not refunded, and you can cancel before the next one at any time. Your statutory rights are not affected.
