Questions

Before you try it.

Do you keep my designs?

Only while you work on them. Delete them any time. We do not train on your uploads.

Can I upload any tattoo?

Only designs you made or have the right to use. We remove material when a rights holder asks.

Does it work on a tablet?

Yes, in the browser. A phone app may follow.

Does it cost anything today?

No. The free tool runs in your browser and nothing is charged. Paid plans are not open yet.
