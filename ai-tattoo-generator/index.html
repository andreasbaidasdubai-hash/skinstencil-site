How it works

  1. Describe it. Write a few words about the subject and the style. Choose Bold for solid lines or Fine detail for delicate linework.
  2. Get the design. The AI draws black line art on white, made to convert cleanly. It takes about 20 seconds.
  3. Make the stencil. One click opens the design in the Studio, where you adjust it, check what changed and print it at true size on A4 or Letter.

Questions

Is the AI tattoo generator free?

You can try it free: visitors get a few designs a day without an account. A day pass or Pro raises the daily limit a lot. Turning a design into a stencil works as in the Studio: free downloads carry a watermark, and a license key removes it.

Can I use the design for a real tattoo?

Treat it as a starting point. Show it to your tattoo artist: they decide whether it works for the body part and size, and they should check that it does not copy someone else’s work.

Why black line art on white?

A stencil is made from clean outlines. Grey shading and backgrounds turn into noise, so the generator asks for plain black lines, and the Studio then shows what the stencil kept.

What happens to my description?

It is sent to our generator service and to the image provider that draws the design, under their own terms. We do not keep your description or the design. Do not write personal details or names in it. The stencil step runs on your own device.
