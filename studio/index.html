Tips for cleaner stencils

  • Photograph or scan flat, in even light, with the design filling the frame.
  • Use Drawing for sketches and flash. Use Photo for pictures with soft shading.
  • Raise Remove specks to clean noise. Lower Sensitivity if shading turns into lines.
  • If lines look faint on your printer, try a heavier line weight and test on paper first.
