Paid plans and license keys

The key is stored only in your browser (local storage on your device). When you activate it, and now and then afterwards, your browser sends the key to the payment provider's license service to check that it is valid. Remove the key from the studio page at any time to delete it from your device.

The hosting provider (GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.)) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and requested page for security and operation.

The fonts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.
