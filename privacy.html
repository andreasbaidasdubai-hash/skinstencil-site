The free tool

The stencil tool at /studio/ runs entirely in your browser. The images you load are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us or stored by us. Closing the page discards them.

The AI design generator

The generator at /ai-tattoo-generator/ is the one part that is not local. The description you type (up to 200 characters) is sent to our generator service, which runs on Cloudflare and forwards it to an image provider (fal.ai, which runs the image models of Recraft and OpenAI) that draws the design. The picture comes back to your browser. We do not keep your description or the design; the providers handle them under their own terms, and we do not control how long they keep them. Do not put personal details, names or photos of people in a description.

You can also add a picture as an example. It is shrunk and re-saved in your browser first, which removes its metadata (such as location and camera), and is then sent through the same service to the same image provider together with your description. It must not show a person: please use drawings, flash or objects, not photos of faces. The “Show it realistic” button sends the finished design and the chosen body part, skin tone and position to the image provider, which draws it onto one of our own stock skin photos; no photo of you is involved. We do not keep any of this either. The stencil, the print sheet and the downloads are made on your device.

To enforce daily limits, the service keeps a counter next to a short one-way hash of your IP address (or of your license key, if you hold one) for up to two days. The hash cannot be turned back into the address. A visitor's license key is also sent to the payment provider's license service to check it is valid.

To tell people from automated scripts, the generator uses Cloudflare Turnstile. It runs in your browser, and Cloudflare, Inc. processes the request, so it sees your IP address and browser signals as any website would. A design you turn into a stencil is handed to the stencil tool through your browser's session storage and is processed on your device from then on.

What we collect

This site does not set cookies. It counts page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors: we see totals such as visits, pages, countries, referrers and device types. Cloudflare, Inc. processes the request to load its counting script, so it sees your IP address as any website would. If we add accounts or other tracking, we will update this page first.

Paid plans and license keys

When paid plans are switched on, payments are handled by our payment provider, which acts as the seller of record and collects your payment details and tax information. We do not see or store your card details. You receive a license key by email.

The key is stored only in your browser (local storage on your device). When you activate it, and now and then afterwards, your browser sends the key to the payment provider's license service to check that it is valid. Remove the key from the studio page at any time to delete it from your device.

The hosting provider (GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.)) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and requested page for security and operation.

The fonts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.
